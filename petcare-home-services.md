# Kata: PetCare Home Services

## Description
A pet care company wants to offer online booking for grooming, veterinary visits, pet walking, boarding, and home visits.

## Users
Pet owners across multiple cities.

## Requirements
- Allow users to create profiles for their pets.
- Allow users to book grooming, walking, boarding, or vet services.
- Allow users to choose pickup/drop-off or home visit when available.
- Accept online payment or payment at the location.
- Notify users about appointment confirmation, reminders, and completion.
- Allow providers to update service status.
- Support national promotions.
- Support local promotions per branch or provider.
- Integrate with mapping services for home visits.
- Support mobile access.
- Allow users to upload vaccination records.
- Allow providers to reject bookings if requirements are not met.

## Additional Context
- Some services require vaccination verification.
- Providers may be employees, contractors, or franchise locations.
- Availability depends on provider schedules and capacity.
- Some pets require special handling.
- The company plans to expand into pet insurance and subscription plans.
